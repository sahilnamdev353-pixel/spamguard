About SpamGuard

SpamGuard is a spam detector for emails and SMS messages, built as a college project with Python and machine learning.

How it works

  1. You paste a message.
  2. The text is converted to numbers with TF-IDF, which scores how important each word is.
  3. A Naive Bayes model, trained on thousands of labelled messages, estimates the chance of spam.
  4. You see the verdict, a confidence score and the words that pushed it towards spam.

What we store

Your name, email, a hashed password (never the password itself), and each message you check with its result. You can delete any check from History.

Limits

The model was trained mostly on English SMS data. Hindi and Hinglish messages may be judged less accurately, and no model is right every time.

Built with

Python, Flask, SQLite, scikit-learn, pandas, joblib.
