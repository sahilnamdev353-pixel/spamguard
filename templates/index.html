Questions

Is the result always right?

No. The model is accurate on its test data but can miss spam or flag a good message. Treat it as a second opinion.

What do you store?

Your name, email, a hashed password, and each message you check with its result. You can delete any check from History.

Does it read my Gmail or phone?

No. You paste the text yourself. Gmail connection is planned for a later version.

Does it work in Hindi or Hinglish?

It was trained mostly on English SMS data, so Hindi and Hinglish results are less reliable.
